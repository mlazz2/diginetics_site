Product: Elijah

What Elijah is,
and what you get from it.

Elijah is Diginetics' always-on intelligence system. It connects to your market data and property records, discovers how your market actually behaves, stress-tests every finding on fresh data, and keeps updating as conditions shift. Underwriting Intelligence is the application we built on Elijah for property firms: the cards, monitors, briefs, and document parsing below. You buy the underwriting outputs. Elijah is what makes them defensible.

Request a sample card Who it's for
The Stack

Two layers: Elijah, then Underwriting Intelligence on top.

Most tools show you charts or generate text. Elijah does something different. It learns the operating rules of your market from historical listing, sales, and property-record data, then checks whether those rules still hold on data the system has not seen yet. Only patterns that survive that check can appear on a client card.

Elijah (the intelligence system)

Continuous market discovery on your feeds and uploads. What drives price in your zip and tier. How days on market respond to the ask, rates, and season. Where out-of-state buyers actually originate. Elijah builds and maintains that library for your market and refreshes it as regimes shift.

Underwriting Intelligence (what you use)

Listing Underwrite Cards, CRE deal audits, Active Listing Monitor, document enrichment, weekly briefs, and portfolio queues. Each deliverable answers a specific decision: Take or Pass, cut or hold, re-target or wait, PASS or WARN at IC. PDF plus secure portal. No black-box score.

That is why it is called Elijah: one intelligence layer that keeps learning your market, with underwriting products built on top so brokers, lenders, and developers get decisions they can defend, not another dashboard to interpret.

Underwriting Intelligence
Deliverables built for specific decisions.

Every card ships for a moment in the workflow: sign the listing, cut the price, re-target buyers, pass on a deal, or brief the IC.

Before the Agreement
Underwrite before you commit.

Walk into every listing appointment or deal review with a defensible call. Elijah produces a card per property with pricing posture, risk flags, and a clear verdict.

Listing Underwrite Card

For every residential listing appointment. PDF plus web link.

  • Pricing band validated on your market comps
  • DOM forecast at the ask vs recommended price
  • Price-cut risk before you sign
  • Buyer-origin feeder ZIPs ranked by tier
  • Broker verdict: Take, Take at price, or Pass

Property enrichment

Parsed records attached to every card automatically.

  • MLS comp pool cleaned for arms-length sales
  • County deed and ownership timeline
  • Probate and estate flags
  • Subdivision investor vs retail mix
  • Seller motivation context from estate filings

Buyer-origin marketing panel

By tier: luxury lakefront, campus corridor, entry SFR, and custom segments.

  • Feeder ZIP rankings for each listing tier
  • Channel recommendations per cohort
  • Seasonality windows (e.g. SEC, UA, migration cycles)
  • Spend guidance tied to what actually closes your tier

CRE Deal Underwrite Card

For commercial listings and acquisitions. Up to two active deals per quarter included.

  • Assumption audit on rent, vacancy, cap rate, exit
  • Market band cross-check vs borrower or sponsor model
  • PASS, WARN, or PARTIAL posture for IC or credit committee
  • 2 to 4 page exhibit export for the deal file

Document Parsing
Parsed records behind every card.

Elijah ingests structured feeds and uploaded documents, extracts the fields that matter for underwriting, and attaches them to each property or deal. No manual comp scrubbing.

MLS / Trestle feeds

Active, pending, and closed listings: price, DOM, cuts, property type, zip, subdivision. Powers comp pools, pricing bands, DOM forecasts, and absorption benchmarks.

County records

Deed type, ownership timeline, assessed value, arms-length filtering. Clean comps, title and distress context, investor vs retail mix in subdivision.

Probate and estate records

Estate filings, admin deeds, inherited-sale signals by property and subdivision. Estate vs retail pricing posture and seller motivation context.

Market indices

Zip-level home values, rent indices, migration and wealth proxies for out-of-state feeder markets. Buyer-origin ranking and cross-check when MLS comps are thin.

Rent rolls and operating statements

Uploaded PDFs and spreadsheets parsed into structured assumptions. Rent growth, vacancy, expense ratios extracted for CRE deal audits.

Appraisals and deal memos

Key assumptions pulled from appraisal excerpts and IC memos. Cross-checked against validated market bands before the vote.
