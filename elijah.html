What Elijah Does

Chatbots hallucinate because they optimize for fluent language. Elijah optimizes for relationships that survive testing on your data. Specialists built on Elijah cannot ship operational answers that were never validated in the client's library.

Finds the drivers

What Elijah Has Already Found
Real findings from real industries.

Same engine, no custom tuning, run on real data. Below are the kind of insights Elijah surfaced on its first pass through these industries. Each one is a readable formula, not a black-box prediction.

Real Estate

Housing supply tracks mortgage rates with nearly perfect accuracy.

Elijah found that months of supply moves almost entirely with the 30-year mortgage rate. Rates rise, supply rises. Held through zero-rate policy, rapid tightening, and plateau. One driver, never broke. The kind of formula a brokerage can plan capacity around for the next 12 months.

Real Estate

Housing permits track new sales at a steady 2 to 1 ratio.

Two permits for every completed new home sale, stable across a full housing cycle. A 10% drop in sales means roughly a 10% permit decline. Builders, REITs, and lenders can read the pipeline months ahead from a single number Elijah surfaced.

Healthcare

30-day readmissions, hit within 0.02 percentage points.

Elijah was given diabetes hospital data with no clinical hypothesis. It independently named the share of patients with high glucose, adjusted for demographics, as the dominant driver. Projected the readmission rate within 0.02 percentage points of actual, when published models reach 2 to 5 points.

Sports

Aaron Judge: 54 home runs forecast vs 53 actual, locked pre-season.

Elijah ran on pre-2025 player data and locked the forecast before Opening Day. The driver it surfaced was short-term power surges, not contact quality. Scouting watches contact. Elijah watches momentum, and got the most important power hitter in baseball within one home run.

Retail

One product category predicts the whole store, three days early.

Elijah found that disposable vape average sale moves three days before the rest of the store follows. One category becomes the early warning system for the entire business, well before standard reports flag anything.

Energy

Operational uptime, not flow rate, drives reservoir output.

Engineers expected flow rate or pressure to dominate. Elijah found uptime had the strongest fit on the industry-standard Volve dataset. A counter-intuitive answer that points operators toward a more controllable lever.

Transportation

Freight and passenger speed gap, captured exactly.

Elijah produced a closed-form formula for the freight-passenger speed gap, AM and PM, then independently replicated it on a separate corridor dataset. DOTs spend years calibrating travel-demand models. Elijah did it in a single run.

Water and Resources

Conflict events predict water stress better than water itself.

In a dataset built around water stress, Elijah surfaced conflict events as the strongest leading signal, ahead of freshwater per capita or rainfall. The kind of cross-domain finding that traditional analysis would never go looking for.
