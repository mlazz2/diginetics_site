The Real Problem
Agents do not need another CRM. They need the targeting layer underneath it.

Most tools help agents move faster. Manifest helps them move correctly: which ZIP to target, which buyer segment to pitch, what price to defend, and what market read to bring into the first meeting.

Target the right buyer

Manifest ranks feeder ZIPs and buyer cohorts for each listing, so marketing spend goes to the people most likely to act.

Pitch the right angle

A Dallas buyer and a Birmingham buyer can see the same listing completely differently. Manifest tells the agent which story fits each segment.

Defend the price

Agents walk into seller meetings with the price floor, rate pressure, supply read, and recommended action, not a generic comp sheet.
