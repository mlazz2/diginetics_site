Our Thesis
Help operators understand their data.

Generalist AI is built to tell you what may happen next. Diginetics is built to help you understand why things happening now are actually happening, and how those forces may repeat or change in the future.

What LLMs optimize for

What may happen next

Fluent guesses from language training. Useful for drafting and exploration. Weak when an operator needs to know why output dropped, why DOM spiked, or why a deal assumption no longer holds.

  • Plausible narrative, not necessarily your market
  • Hard to verify against your actual history
  • Answers the forecast before it answers the mechanism
What Elijah optimizes for

Why it is happening now

Operating realities in your data: the relationships that drive price, days on market, absorption, buyer origin, and deal assumptions. Located, tested on fresh data, and written so your team can read and challenge them.

  • Why this listing is stalling at this ask
  • Which drivers actually moved the number
  • How the same forces may repeat or break when conditions shift
Markets and businesses obey realities the same way physics does. An object in motion tends to stay in motion. Once you understand that law, brakes on a car are a natural placement. Without it, you only learn stopping the hard way: crash first, understand later. Elijah locates those operating laws in your data so decisions like Take or Pass, cut or hold, and re-target or wait follow naturally. You do not have to expire the listing to learn what the market already showed you.

Document Parsing

Every card starts with clean property context.

Elijah ingests and parses the records behind each deal: MLS listing history, county deeds, probate filings, and subdivision composition. No manual comp scrubbing. No guessing on seller motivation.

How document parsing works
  • MLS / Trestle feeds: active, pending, and closed listings. Price, DOM, cuts, property type, zip, subdivision.
  • County records: deed type, ownership timeline, assessed value, arms-length sale filtering for clean comps.
  • Probate and estate filings: admin deeds, inherited-sale signals by property and subdivision.
  • Market indices: zip-level values, rent indices, migration proxies for out-of-state feeder markets.
  • Your uploads: rent rolls, operating statements, appraisal excerpts, and deal memos parsed into structured assumptions.

What Elijah is not: a CRM replacement, lead pipeline, email system, or historical data migration project. It is full-cycle underwriting intelligence: underwrite before sign, guide pricing and DOM after sign, until close. Your team keeps working in Matrix, Argus, or whatever stack you run today.
