What Universal Equations Are

Mathematical relationships independently discovered across separate entities and confirmed to hold structurally. These are not statistical averages — they are structurally identical symbolic equations discovered independently and then validated across the population.

Why They Matter

When universal equations are fed back into individual analyses as seed equations, they dramatically accelerate convergence and improve projection quality — particularly for entities with limited historical data. Every new domain strengthens these laws for everyone.
