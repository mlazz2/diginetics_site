Security

Built for environments
that cannot leak data.

Specialist AI only works if clients trust the boundary around their operation. Diginetics deploys in isolated environments with encryption, audit trails, and guardrails designed for regulated and high-stakes industries from the start.

Tenant isolation

Each client deployment runs in a dedicated environment on AWS. Client data, validated libraries, and specialist outputs stay inside the client's boundary. There is no shared operational memory across clients.

Encryption at rest

Data stored in client environments is encrypted at rest. Discovery runs, validation artifacts, and forecast records are persisted as reproducible, auditable outputs.

No public-model exposure

Operational answers do not draw from public internet training data. Specialists retrieve from the client's validated library only. Client IP is not exposed to third-party model providers as part of normal operation.

Audit trails

Every recommendation can be traced to validated relationships in the client's environment. Teams, auditors, and compliance officers can inspect the logic behind any output.

Guardrails by architecture

Relationships that fail validation do not reach production. The system is designed so operational facts cannot ship unless they exist in the client's validated library.

Enterprise diligence

Detailed security documentation, architecture reviews, and compliance questionnaires are available for enterprise partners during procurement and diligence.

Privacy
Your data stays yours.

Diginetics processes client data solely to discover, validate, and deploy intelligence within the client's isolated environment. We do not sell client data. We do not use client operational data to train shared models for other customers. Validated relationships compound inside the client's private library, not in a cross-client pool.

Data minimization

We work with the operational data required for discovery and deployment. Data handling scope is defined per engagement and documented in client agreements.

Retention and deletion

Retention policies are configured per client environment. Deletion and export requests are handled according to the client's contract and applicable law.
