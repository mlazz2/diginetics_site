Go-To-Market
The YOYO Plan

Most enterprise GTM is horizontal (500 cold logos) or land-and-expand inside one account only. YOYO combines depth on one operator with lift through the shared vendors and integrators that serve the rest of the industry. The yoyo string is the hub, not a generic channel reseller.

Down

Go deep on one flagship operator. Map their stack. Build auditable decision logic on their data in an isolated tenant.

→
Across

Up

Expand to peers

Sell to sibling operators through those hubs. Proof already built. Decision templates transfer. Client data never commingles.

Why YOYO fits this company

Explainable logic travels. Black-box scores do not.

We deploy readable relationships, not opaque predictions. A hub can co-sell the same decision layer to every operator it touches because regulators and partners can inspect the mechanism. That is why YOYO is the GTM architecture for mechanistic AI, not a sales tactic pasted on top of chat.
