Why Agents Use It
Manifest turns every meeting into a market-read conversation.

Buyer agreements, price cuts, dead leads, CRM blasts, and listing pitches all point to the same missing layer: agents need better market intelligence at the moment they are talking to a client.

01

Before a buyer meeting

Generate a one-page brief: 12-month price read, rate window, max-bid impact, and the 90-day plan to be ready.

02

Before a listing pitch

Show the seller which buyer cohorts fit, what price range the market will engage, and where a cut becomes necessary.

03

Before a campaign launch

Rank feeder ZIPs and messages so the agent does not spend money advertising the right listing to the wrong audience.

04

Before agent coaching

Team leaders see who is targeting correctly, who is cutting too early, and who needs help turning data into a client script.

Use Cases
Built around the moments that decide commission.
Listing agents

Stop guessing when a price cut is needed.

Manifest separates a bad price from a bad audience. A listing can look overpriced locally while the real buyers are sitting three feeder markets away.

  • Price floor and engagement range by listing type
  • Feeder ZIP ranking for luxury, SFR, and relocation demand
  • Hold, adjust, or retarget recommendations before days on market pile up
Buyer agents

Justify value in the first 10 minutes.

Post-settlement buyer agreements force agents to prove value earlier. Manifest gives the agent the market read before the first showing.

  • Projected home-price movement for the target metro
  • Rate scenario and max-bid impact
  • A 90-day buyer plan that feels consultative, not transactional
Team leads

Coach the segment problem, not the symptom.

Dead leads are often not dead. They were pitched the wrong property, at the wrong time, with the wrong message.

  • Sort leads by origin ZIP, viewed property type, and likely buyer psychology
  • Separate wealth-arbitrage buyers from affordability-escape buyers
  • Give each agent a message matrix instead of a generic drip
Broker owners

See which agents have a real market strategy.

Brokerages do not just need more CRM activity. They need to know if agents are targeting the right people and defending the right advice.

  • Agent-by-agent listing launch quality
  • Marketing waste by geography and segment
  • Price-cut risk and coaching triggers by office

How It Fits
Manifest does not replace your CRM. It tells the CRM what to do.

Command, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, BoldTrail, and similar tools move the workflow. Manifest sits underneath them as the targeting and market-intelligence layer: which contact, which listing, which message, which timing.

CRM input

Contacts, source ZIPs, saved searches, property views, price bands, agent notes, and listing inventory.

Manifest layer

Segment fit, feeder ZIP rank, listing price defense, buyer brief, rate sensitivity, and campaign recommendation.

CRM output

Better follow-up, better drip logic, better listing campaigns, better agent coaching, and more defensible client conversations.
